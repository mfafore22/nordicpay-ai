NordicPay Basic account has a monthly fee of €0. Includes 1 debit card, 5 free ATM withdrawals per month, and mobile banking.

NordicPay Plus account has a monthly fee of €4.90. Includes 2 cards, unlimited ATM withdrawals, travel insurance, and priority support.

NordicPay Premium account has a monthly fee of €14.90. Includes all Plus features plus concierge service, airport lounge access, and premium metal card.

ATM withdrawal fees: Basic account €2 per withdrawal after 5 free ones. Plus and Premium have unlimited free withdrawals.

Account upgrades: You can upgrade from Basic to Plus or Premium anytime in the app under Settings > Account > Upgrade. Downgrades take effect next billing cycle.

Card activation: Activate your new card in the NordicPay app under Cards > Activate. You will need to enter the last 4 digits and set your PIN.

Change PIN: Open the NordicPay app, go to Cards > Your Card > Change PIN. You can also change it at any NordicPay ATM.

Card freeze: Open the app, go to Cards > Your Card > Freeze. This instantly blocks all transactions. Unfreeze anytime.

Cancel subscription: Go to Settings > Subscriptions > select the subscription > Cancel. Cancellation is effective immediately.

Password reset: Go to the login page, click Forgot Password, enter your email. You will receive a reset link valid for 24 hours.

Account opening: Takes 5-10 minutes online. You need a valid ID and Finnish residence. Non-residents can apply with a valid passport and proof of address.

Account closure: Free of charge. Go to Settings > Account > Close Account. Any remaining balance will be transferred to your specified account.

Quick Loan: Maximum amount €25,000. Decision in 60 seconds. Interest rate from 4.9% APR. Terms from 1-7 years.

Home Renovation Loan: Up to €50,000. Terms 1-10 years. No collateral needed for amounts under €20,000. Interest from 3.9% APR.

All loans: No early repayment penalty. You can pay off your loan at any time without extra charges.

Loan requirements: Minimum credit score of 600. Must be 18+ with regular income. Finnish residence required.

Data collection: We collect name, address, transaction history, device information, and usage patterns for service improvement.

Data sharing: Credit agencies for loan applications. Payment processors for transactions. Law enforcement when legally required. Never sold to third parties.

Data security: All data encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Two-factor authentication available on all accounts.

Data retention: Transaction data kept for 7 years as required by Finnish law. Account data deleted 90 days after account closure.

Fraud protection: Zero liability for unauthorized transactions reported within 48 hours. 24/7 fraud monitoring on all accounts.

Service outage compensation: If services are down for more than 4 hours, affected customers receive a €5 credit per incident.

Multiple cards: Basic allows 1 card. Plus allows up to 2 cards. Premium allows up to 5 cards. Additional cards €2/month each.

Update email: Go to Settings > Profile > Email. Verify with your current email before the change takes effect.
